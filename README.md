# Bank_Churn_Analysis

## Project Summary

This project analyzes **bank customer churn** to understand why customers leave the bank and which segments are more likely to churn. The dataset contains information about **10,000 bank customers**, including their age, location, account balance, number of products, and account activity.

### Key Objectives

* Identify patterns behind **customer churn**
* Understand how **demographics, geography, and account behavior** affect churn
* Discover **high-risk customer segments** for better retention strategies

### Tools Used

* **Power BI** – to build interactive dashboards and visualize insights
* **Excel / CSV** – for initial data preparation and cleaning
* **DAX** – to calculate important metrics such as churn rate and segmentation measures

These tools were used because they allow efficient data analysis and help present insights in a **clear, interactive, and business-friendly format**.

### Key Insights

* Customers with **1 product show the highest churn (~27%)**, while customers with **2 products have much lower churn (~7%)**, indicating stronger loyalty among multi-product users.
* **Germany shows the highest churn rate among regions**, suggesting potential retention challenges in that market.
* **Senior customers have the highest churn levels**, while elder customers show relatively lower churn.
* **Inactive customers are significantly more likely to churn compared to active members**, highlighting the importance of customer engagement.

### Outcome

The dashboard helps identify **key drivers of customer churn and high-risk customer segments**, enabling banks to design **better customer retention strategies and improve long-term customer relationships**.
